# Social profiles, contact and promotion features

Use with [social publishing](social-publishing.md), which owns identity research, clickable routes,
[hashtags](social-publishing.md#search-wording-and-hashtag-placement) and
[pins and featured collections](social-publishing.md#keep-significant-posts-easy-to-find).
Keep brand-specific accounts, copy, approvals and observations in that project's guide.

## Inspect before changing

Research establishes options, not authorization or account eligibility. Apply only the current
one-time or standing scope; a research request does not authorize profile edits, follows, Stories,
messages, paid upgrades or held media publication. Work on 1 destination at a time.

Confirm the acting account, role and current fields, links/order, placements, permissions and cost.
Preserve useful wording, evergreen links and existing relationships. Describe present services
accurately; proposed services stay plans. Keep the bio balanced across the organization’s actual
purpose and current capabilities while adding useful, high-impact offerings missing from it.
Do not let a recent campaign erase its broader work. Preserve any separate approval requirement
for promotional wording placed outside the post. Keep an approved public business email reachable where
someone monitors it. Prefer a dedicated contact field when available; use the bio or display name
as a fallback when space requires it. Preserve an established domain handle and verify that the
contact route is usable. Never invent an office, hours, coverage promise or personal details to
complete a profile.

For an authorized cross-link audit, compare each verified official profile with the other official
accounts. Inspect all suitable native link, social and About fields and their actual limits; use
available capacity for useful routes without duplicate destinations or misleading labels. Follow
the brand’s approved ordering and retained destinations, preserving core bio meaning and public
contact. If every account cannot fit, prioritize useful clickable routes and record omissions with
the observed limit. Verify every saved destination from the visitor’s view; an example of a possible
missing link is a check to perform, not evidence of a defect. Keep project timing and account lists
in the project’s plan.

Match each placement to its reader task: direct media link, profile selector, channel, Story,
Highlight or featured collection. Follow the publishing guide's route and saved-result checks.
Check the actual free route before considering a paid feature; broad marketing copy does not settle
eligibility. A desktop omission may have a supported mobile route, not a missing account feature.

## Platform evidence and account checks

Sources reviewed October 21, 2026. Dates below identify historical announcements where relevant.
Recheck changing features against current official help and the actual account before execution.

### Instagram

Inspect bio, category, link labels/order and intended business contact fields. Exact limits and
available controls need native confirmation. Follow the publishing guide’s reader-goal rule
when choosing a direct milestone or collection link; keep useful evergreen links.
[Business contact tools](https://about.fb.com/ja/news/2016/08/instagram_businesstool/)
were announced in 2016; that release does not establish current setup or eligibility.

[Link stickers](https://about.fb.com/ja/news/2021/10/linkaccessstickers/) support external Story links
(2021 announcement). [Highlights](https://about.fb.com/ja/news/2017/12/instagram_highlight_archive/)
retain Stories beyond 24 hours (2017 announcement). A feed-post reshare opens the post, so inspect
whether a separate link sticker is needed for the media route. Verify the actual saved Story and
Highlight link; these sources do not establish that every archived sticker works in every account.

A disabled outer **Add link** control in Meta Business Suite does not settle Story-link support.
In an Instagram-only Story composer observed October 21, 2026, **Edit > More stickers > Create link
sticker** accepted a URL and custom label; the published sticker opened the intended destination.
Inspect that supported editor route when available, verify canvas placement and test the live link.
Treat this as observed account behavior, not a promise that every account or format has it.

Inspect Account Status/recommendation eligibility, search-engine visibility and account suggestions
before blaming poor discovery on hashtags. Current primary help for these controls was unavailable
in this review; establish exact eligibility, choices and current state natively. Eligibility is not
promised reach. Do not import private contacts to improve discovery.

Do not assert that Instagram never permits clickable post links: [Meta One's September 2026
announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai/)
lists organic post/Reel links among paid Advanced features, varying by app, region and account.
An actual plain-text caption still needs a verified free route; do not buy access without approval.

### Facebook

Inspect Page intro/About, category, website, public contact and available
[action-button choices](https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/1638565256396310?locale=en_GB).
Switch into the Page; do not substitute a member's profile action. Select a useful destination from
actual choices rather than assuming a particular button label exists.

[Page access](https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410/r.php/) covers content, Stories, inbox and
comments. Inspect the current Page Story/reshare editor and test its saved link. Page identity is
separate from member identity in the [2021 Page experience](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/01/introducing-the-new-page-experience/).
That announcement does not establish Page-follow support for every ordinary personal profile;
confirm the actor and exact destination's supported action.

### LinkedIn organization Pages

[Page fields](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a542801) and
[Edit page](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564346/edit-your-linkedin-page-or-showcase-page?lang=en)
support accurate organization information. [Free versus Premium CTA help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6291123)
places free custom buttons under More; Premium adds prominent placements. Inspect the free option
and current destination before proposing an upgrade; Page CTA click counts are available in analytics.

[Organization following](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1398264) uses Settings >
Manage following > Add Pages to follow, for other organization Pages. Personal-profile actions
remain member actions; use the publishing guide's account-identity exception. Public-post
[Page comments/reactions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a700698) have a separate actor picker.
[Page messaging](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1469428) lets admins reply after a
member initiates contact; enabling an inbox does not create unsolicited outreach permission.

Use Featured and eligible [posts/reposts](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a567075).
[Stories for Pages ended in 2021](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/linkedin-pages/organic-stories).
An indexed old Stories guide is not current capability evidence.

### X and Bluesky

[X profile help](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-customize-your-profile) documents
name, header, location, website and a 160-character bio. Accurate profile wording helps readers and
[account search](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-search-not-working); it does not guarantee ranking.
[Message requests](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/direct-messages) and
[reply controls](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-conversations) need separate inspection.

[Bluesky domain handles](https://bsky.social/about/blog/04-21-2025-verification) support organization
identity and differ from verification badges. [Custom feeds](https://bsky.social/about/blog/7-27-2023-custom-feeds)
have their own inclusion rules; following a feed does not distribute a post into it.
[Starter packs](https://bsky.social/about/blog/06-26-2024-starter-packs) are editorial collections,
not a reason to mass-follow. [DM controls](https://bsky.social/about/blog/05-22-2024-direct-messages)
were documented in 2024; inspect current settings rather than assuming launch defaults.
No native Stories capability was established by these sources.

### TikTok

The [dedicated profile-link guide](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account)
specifies 1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account for website links; supported social
connections vary by region. A basic Business Account switch does not prove registration or link
eligibility. Prefer this specific guidance and the saved account screen over broader
[account-type descriptions](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/switching-to-a-creator-or-business-account).

[Stories](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/tiktok-stories) last 24 hours.
[Creator Playlists](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-playlist) are not
available to every account; inspect actual controls without inventing a threshold or pin limit.
[Reuse controls](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/account-privacy-settings/duets)
can couple Duet, Stitch, stickers and Story reuse. [Downloads](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/account-privacy-settings/video-downloads)
are separate; disabling them does not remove existing copies. Review rights and context before
changing either. Account suggestions/contact sync and permission to receive messages are separate.

### YouTube

[Profile links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2657964) support up to 14, with the first
prominent; capacity is not a target. [Home layout](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3219384)
distinguishes a trailer for non-subscribers, returning-viewer Spotlight and up to 12 custom sections.
A watched feature may stop appearing to that viewer; it is not a universal permanent pin.

[Related video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157) gives Shorts a clickable route to
a public/unlisted video on the same channel, requiring advanced-feature access. Verify that access
and the saved placement. [Link-location rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639)
say Shorts description/comment URLs and Post URLs are not clickable; a pinned Short comment is not
a workaround. Profile/Related video links are clickable; long-form external links have eligibility rules.

Confirm the active [channel identity](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3046356) before a
subscription. [Private subscriptions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7280190) can still be
real; do not expose the full list merely to prove a follow. [Messaging](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16650958)
has a limited rollout, including the US, with age, account and mobile-app requirements; Brand Account
access is owner-only. Do not claim universal absence or availability. Public business contact remains
separate. Inspect each video's [comments](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9483359) and
[remix controls](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10623810); new-upload defaults do not prove older settings.

## Contact, following and measurement

For each later authorized follow, confirm the platform-specific identity and actor, then record
already following, followed/subscribed, request pending, unsupported or no verified match. A request
is not acceptance; paid subscription/membership is not a free follow. No employer substitution,
contact upload, private message or mass-follow follows from a tag or a research request.

Keep legitimate discussion accessible with a staffed inbox and targeted moderation.
[Meta's 2024 safety guide](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/06/protecting-athletes-and-fans-on-our-apps-during-major-sporting-events-this-summer/)
documents Hidden Words, requests, Limits, Restrict and tag/mention controls. Review sharing, profile
tag display, comments and inbox requests separately; blanket link filtering can hide useful sources.
Inbound permission is not outreach authorization. Check cross-post destinations independently.

Capture a baseline and comparable time windows with actual metric names: visitors, link taps,
replies, follows and watch behavior are different outcomes. Do not infer causal lift from 1 post.
Use available native analytics; no new paid tool or recurring monitor is implied. TikTok
[Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/creative/creativeCenter/trends) post/view counts differ from
[Search Insights](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights) popularity signals. YouTube
[Trends](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757) depends on availability and data;
[Shorts views](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070) include starts/replays, while engaged
views, retention and watch time answer different questions. Preserve units and uncertainty.
