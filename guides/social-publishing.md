# Shared social publishing practice

Use this guide alongside the project's own writing, accounts and publishing guide. This file owns
methods that apply across projects; it does not own any organization's voice, people or post approvals.
Video limits live in [social-video-upload-limits.md](social-video-upload-limits.md).

## Evidence behind publishing advice

Keep 3 kinds of evidence distinct when adopting or changing a rule:

- **Platform documentation:** link the official source, when it was read and the
  account/format it covers. A documented feature or recommendation is not measured
  improvement in reach, search ranking or inquiries.
- **Own observation:** record the account type, format, date and saved result.
  A successful action proves that case, not a rule for every account or platform.
- **Experiment:** record the question, comparison, outcome measure, period and
  approved cost before running it. Preserve failed and inconclusive results. Keep
  changes in audience, timing, recrawl and product mode visible; a before/after
  change alone does not establish what caused it.

Missing measurements stay unknown. Do not turn workshop advice, a successful post
or an anecdote into a ranking claim. Hand typing or removing provenance is not
proof of greater reach. Keep the project's
approved disclosures and each destination's required labels; disclose a material
fact when its omission would mislead the reader.

Use the project's approved destination and publication order. A website-first
step requires an approved, working destination; a held section is not a condition
invented for an otherwise approved post. Test the actual reader route before
publication and retain any meaningful uncertainty in captions and source text.

## Prepare the actual destination

- Identify the intended platform, account, post format and approved publication scope. Read the
  connected accounts rather than assuming every requested platform is connected. Drafting for several
  platforms does not authorize posting to all of them.
  Check official service pricing before choosing a paid connector: free connected-account slots
  and platform usage fees are separate. Do not disconnect another account expecting usage fees to
  disappear. A native route can avoid connector/API charges within the authorized scope; it does
  not authorize paid features or expanded posting. [Zernio pricing](https://zernio.com/pricing) and
  [Adeli pricing](https://www.tryadeli.com/docs/pricing) separate account charges from X usage;
  [Adeli's X guide](https://www.tryadeli.com/docs/api/posts/publish-x) confirms that X posts incur
  API charges. These sources were read October 21, 2026; recheck rather than treating rates as fixed.
- Before preparing each platform, assess its useful promotion options on the actual account:
  formats, link treatment, profile placements and collections, relevant mentions or collaboration
  features, and search wording or hashtags. Compare native capabilities with the connected tool.
  Select what serves the intended audience using current evidence, not availability alone;
  state cost, space and content tradeoffs, uncertainty and any paid or account-specific gate.
  Honor the project's explicit standing approval: when it covers promotion within an authorized
  future post, research, select and apply suitable tactics without asking again for each tactic.
  Otherwise recommend them within the current scope. Record useful choices and supported exceptions.
  Standing approval is project-specific; it does not independently authorize new posts or recipients,
  retroactive changes, paid upgrades, ads or recurring paid work, and never overrides a review hold.
  Verify applied tactics in the native saved result and preserve useful existing placements.
  Revisit useful features and research within each authorized posting task, incorporating new
  evidence and available measured feedback on engagement, reach and discovery. Inspect covers,
  crops, text and captions at phone size for readable, coherent visual presentation. Choose features
  that serve the reader; using every available control is not a goal. Keep decisions, successful
  solutions and failures current across applicable platforms without creating recurring work.
- Confirm current text and media limits for the account and format from official guidance or the
  platform's own validation. Count the final text, including links, line breaks and tags as that
  platform counts them. Keep dated limit evidence separate from lasting writing rules.
  Use verified event-location metadata when supported; otherwise name the place in text when
  useful. Never substitute the operator’s current location for where the content was recorded.
- Adapt the complete message for each destination. Keep facts, qualifications, meaningful uncertainty,
  required links and the reader's next action. Treat titles, captions, video and previews as 1 message.
  Choose the shortest verified tappable or clickable route that serves the approved reader goal
  and brand destination priority for each account and format. A website-traffic or sharing goal
  does not establish search-ranking gains; do not equate clicks with improved ranking. Preserve an explicitly selected channel or library link when the goal
  includes browsing a collection; do not replace it merely to save clicks. Keep the canonical media
  or replay-page URL visible alongside supported tap directions so readers recognize where the
  content lives, within the format's actual space and link rules. For Stories with an approved
  single primary destination, 1 clearly labelled tappable link is sufficient; omit redundant printed
  URLs and secondary destinations. Keep this format-specific choice separate from feed captions
  and profile links.
  When giving directions through a YouTube profile link, put those directions first, followed by
  “Also available on our website:” and the canonical website replay URL, adapting the possessive
  to the publishing account. This ordering applies to that route, not to unrelated direct-link posts.
  A standalone unclickable address is not a useful watch action when a working native route is
  available. If the post cannot link directly, use a verified profile link or other native
  placement and describe the actual steps, including a link selector when the profile has several
  links. Use the saved link's exact label and say where it leads. A channel link is not a direct
  video link: verify the video is available there, identify it clearly and include the needed
  selection rather than promising 1-click playback. Prefer a direct milestone link when supported
  and consistent with the approved reader goal; do not block a valid existing route on a deferred
  profile change. Do not promise “link in bio” until its saved label and destination are verified
  from the reader's view. Recheck the saved
  call to action and route after an authorized edit; existing publication and review holds still apply.
  Solve routine format constraints within the approved work: resize, pad or crop existing approved
  media without regenerating it, preserving required text, faces, meaning and source files. Crop only
  when those requirements remain intact; resampling is not pixel-lossless. Verify the actual preview
  and saved output where available. Preserve useful source, final and reusable media in the project’s
  approved remote and local media destinations, using non-destructive versioned names. Verify each
  location, byte count and matching file hash before recording backup complete; scratch or
  repository-only copies are insufficient. Keep rejected generated trials, unrelated screenshots
  and private data outside publishable media, and preserve explicitly removed/archive states unless
  restoration is authorized. Backup does not publish or approve use. The project owns destinations
  and receipts. Do not call preparation finished while a feasible authorized fix remains, or turn
  an editor-preview crop into an unsupported claim about published output.
- Verify a person's identity and the role of each linked account. A matching name or photo alone is
  insufficient when several accounts exist. Prefer an official website link or a confirmed cross-link.

## Create real name links

Use the destination's supported mention picker or documented connector capability when a name should
be clickable. Select the matching result; plain `@name` text or a pasted Markdown/HTML link does not
prove the platform created a mention. Read the result's identity and account type before selecting it.

Check the requested account types separately on each platform. A campaign/company account found
on 1 platform, or an empty search there, does not settle its presence on another. Link the same
destination once unless a repeated link serves a clear reader need.

Preserve the approved required account types and any approved fallbacks across platform adaptations.
When an account type cannot be verified or supported, apply the approved fallback without asking
again. If neither the required treatment nor its fallback can be completed, make that gap explicit
before publishing and ask only for a choice that changes the approved treatment. Do not silently
substitute a repeated personal mention or unrelated employer. Keep independent preparation moving.

If search returns nothing, try the verified exact page name or username and let the lookup finish.
Keep the search bounded: after reasonable native name/handle searches and official cross-link review
produce no verified match, stop retrying and apply the approved fallback. Reopen only for new useful
evidence; easy matches on other platforms do not justify prolonged searches here or prove absence.
An empty result alone does not establish a disabled-mentions setting. Do not substitute a look-alike
account or claim a name link works when only a visible address remains. Record a real limitation and
keep the correct destination when the platform cannot support the requested treatment.

## Search wording and hashtag placement

Assess discovery for each platform before drafting: useful plain-language keywords, relevant topic
or location hashtags, and whether they belong in the post, caption, description or a comment.
Optimize relevant hashtag use on every applicable platform, especially Instagram, TikTok and X.
Assess a useful broad topic tag alongside 1–2 narrower, specific multiword tags; select the mix that
fits the content, platform guidance, current limits and available space rather than filling a quota.
Where native metrics are available, seek relevant narrower topics with activity in the thousands or
tens of thousands instead of defaulting only to million-volume tags. This is a research preference,
not a proven optimal range. Sample topic fit and recent activity; a small count does not make a stale
or unrelated tag useful, and a relevant broad tag can remain. Inspect current meanings, restrictions
and displayed counts. Record each count's exact unit (posts, views or searches), plus
its time range and geography when shown; post or view counts are not numbers of people searching.
Do not invent search volume or treat activity volume as expected reach. If metrics are unavailable,
record that limit and use supported relevance evidence without blocking the post on missing counts.
Prioritize meaningful content and required links over tag stuffing, and recount the exact final text.
Separate documented search/index behavior from measured reach, clicks or follows. Use available
native analytics on later comparable posts where useful, without inferring cause from 1 post or
starting a recurring monitor or paid test. A searchable tag is not proof of increased reach.
Do not use comment tags as automatic overflow or claim they boost the parent post without evidence.
Keep unknown effects unknown. Apply suitable choices under explicit post-specific or standing
approval; a research question alone does not authorize live edits, comments or new posts.

## LinkedIn through Zernio

Checked October 21, 2026 against Zernio's [mention resolver](https://docs.zernio.com/linkedin-mentions/get-linkedin-mentions)
and [LinkedIn posting guide](https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/linkedin/posts); recheck before reuse.

- Resolve a verified profile URL with its exact LinkedIn display name. Embed the returned
  `mentionFormat` directly in post content. The separate `mentions` array is reference data and
  does not create a mention. Ordinary Markdown links are not a substitute for the returned format.
- A person lookup needs an account administering an organization. A failed lookup does not prove
  that the public profile is absent or cannot be mentioned through another supported route.
- Zernio documents a 3,000-character text limit. Validate the final connector payload; retain both
  source and displayed-text counts when mention markup makes them differ, without assuming which
  count a platform uses.
- A URL in a text post without media can create a preview card. Identify the intended preview URL
  before publishing and inspect the saved title and destination. Placing that URL first succeeded
  in a recorded case; it is not a guarantee of preview selection for every multi-link post.
- Published-post edits replace text and preserve the post ID. The documented edit route cannot
  replace media, articles, polls or reshare targets. Set the intended preview and media before posting.

### Account identity for follows and comments

Checked October 21, 2026. An organization Page can follow another organization Page through its
admin view: **Settings > Manage following > Add Pages to follow**, select the correct Page, then
**Done**. Confirm the acting organization before submitting. This is separate from a member's
follow. [LinkedIn's Page-follow instructions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1398264)
cover this route.

An organization Page cannot follow or connect with a personal profile; that profile's **Follow** or
**Connect** action uses the signed-in member's identity. The [Digital Culture Network LinkedIn Q&A](https://digitalculturenetwork.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/A-beginners-guide-to-LinkedIn-QA-transcript.pdf)
explains this distinction on page 8. Do not substitute a member action when the request is to act
as the organization.

For public-post reactions and comments, LinkedIn has a separate identity picker beside the profile
picture under the post: select the Page and **Save**. [LinkedIn's Page interaction instructions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a700698)
cover that picker. Posting a mention or selecting a comment identity does not change the identity
used by **Follow** or **Connect** on an individual's profile. Verify the actor for each action and
confirm the saved result; opening a profile, picker or invitation form does not prove a follow or
request was sent.

## Publish, edit and verify

Prefer the connected publishing tool for supported actions. If a required feature is unavailable,
use the platform's own editor within the same authorized account and post. Do not create a duplicate
post to work around an editing limitation. Confirm native capabilities before treating a connector
format restriction or failed upload as a platform-wide limit. After a failed upload, establish
whether a post or draft was created before retrying through another route.

Before native browser typing or pasting, establish exclusive input ownership of the app across
active tasks. Coordinate a pause or separate supported surface when another task uses the same
app; recheck the intended account, composer and focused field immediately before input. Do not
continue typing into a changed destination.

Honor standing approval within its task and tool limits. It does not waive a required action-time
human confirmation for legally binding terms. Record the specific confirmation and resulting
action without retaining credentials or verification codes.

Before editing a live post, read its current text, links, tags and media. Connector records can lag
behind native edits. Change only what the request requires and preserve later refinements. When
working through a changing editor, read fresh state after inserting a mention before selecting the
next text; old offsets or stale selections can change the wrong passage. If targeted selection
corrupts an unsaved caption, recover with the complete verified text, resolve each mention again
through the supported picker, and verify the entire caption before saving. Do not save a corrupted
draft or assume visible mention text retained its link after replacement. Verify the full caption
after each mention selection or paste; editors can change focus or spacing. Recheck all selected
accounts after changing a multi-destination picker, including an attempted deselection.

Before calling posting complete, assess relevant promotion features, apply suitable choices within
the granted scope and verify their saved placement. Record why a relevant feature was skipped or
blocked rather than silently leaving the assessment unfinished. Include suitable Story reshares
and persistent collections where they help visitors discover and explore the post, using the
placement guidance below; a feed publication alone does not complete an approved promotion round.

Verify the saved post, not just its draft or a successful tool response: correct account and status,
text, media, preview, name labels, actual profile destinations and other links. Exercise the intended
media behavior and playback; a visible thumbnail or photo does not prove that a video plays or that
the viewer stays on the intended post. Reopen as needed to confirm persistence. A scheduled post is
scheduled, not live. Record the published address or schedule,
connector ID when available, edits and any unresolved checks. Resolve shortened links to verify their
actual destinations. A content/link-tree inspection proves text and destinations, not a complete
visual review; record that limit when screenshots or layout remain uninspected. Never store access
tokens or callbacks.

### X multiple links and edited revisions

Observed October 21, 2026: an X draft showed the intended later URL's preview, but its published
post selected the first URL. Moving the intended preview URL first through the same post's edit
action corrected the live card. Put the desired preview URL first when suitable, then inspect the
published card's title, image and actual destination; this observed solution is not a guarantee of
preview selection, and a draft preview alone does not prove the published result.

When the account offers post editing, use it for a scoped correction and preserve approved wording
and destinations. An edit can create a new revision URL while the old version stays accessible and
points to the newer version. Follow and verify the latest revision, retain both identifiers in the
post record, and distinguish edit history from a duplicate post. Do not assume every account can edit.

### Remove the exact post through a supported route

Recheck the account and post identifier immediately before a destructive action. If a viewer
advances to another post automatically, stop acting on that viewer and use a stable management row
that identifies the intended post. Never delete the next item merely because the menu is still open.

Observed October 21, 2026: TikTok Studio disabled privacy and Delete controls on a post marked
Promotional content, with an app-only notice for commercial content. When that notice applies, use
the signed-in TikTok mobile app on the intended account. Do not remove or misstate a required
commercial disclosure to bypass the restriction, or assume every TikTok deletion requires a phone.
If the agent cannot reach the app, provide only the phone steps needed for the authorized deletion:
open the correct profile, open the exact post, use its More options menu, choose Delete and confirm
the intended item. Ask for completion, then refresh the native/public profile and verify that exact
post is absent while unrelated posts remain. Prefer actual rows/items over a stale total. Record
public removal separately from permanent purge or recovery behavior that was not verified.

## Keep significant posts easy to find

Use [social profile promotion](social-profile-promotion.md) for profile fields, contact routes,
Stories, company-account following and dated platform evidence; this section owns pin/feature choices.

For every authorized post, assess useful native secondary surfaces: Story reshares, persistent
Highlights, playlists, collections, featured and pin placements. Apply relevant options according
to actual account capability and visitor value, retaining useful Stories in supported persistent
collections. Make posts easier to discover and browse without forcing a scarce pin replacement.
Apply them within the post's one-time or standing approval. Verify the actual account and acting
identity, supported format, occupied slots and current limits before adding, reordering or replacing.
Preserve working destination links and the approved reader goal; test the saved content and link
behavior. Names such as “pin”, “feature” and “Highlight” do not establish identical behavior,
persistence or expiry across platforms.
For profile links, inspect existing destinations and ordering before editing. Preserve useful
long-term links, give the current milestone a clear title and assess prominent placement within
actual capacity. Replacing a useful link requires the same visitor-benefit comparison as a scarce
pin slot; verify the saved destination rather than inferring success from the edit form.

In an additive collection, retain useful milestones when another is added. A new featured item does
not by itself justify removing an older one. If a placement is full, or has only 1 scarce slot,
recommend replacement only when the new significant milestone better serves visitors; identify what
would move and why. Consider reordering where supported. Reassess placement when preparing later
significant posts, not through automatic aging or an assumed expiry.

Keep placement changes separate from deleting posts. Do not automatically remove a useful milestone
or delete its underlying post. Apply placements within explicit one-time or standing approval;
without that scope, do not extend a placement action to other platforms or future posts. Verify the
saved placement on the intended account and preserve any unrelated featured items.
Check automatic side effects before toggling a second feature: an X pin action has also confirmed
addition to Highlights. Treat that as observed account behavior, not a universal guarantee; do not
undo an automatic addition by selecting an add/remove toggle without reading its current state.

LinkedIn organization Pages support up to **6 featured posts** in the Home tab's Featured section,
according to [LinkedIn's Page feature guide](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a5944017),
checked October 21, 2026. Page super-admin access is required; job, event and targeted posts are
excluded. This is an additive collection with a limit, not an unlimited collection or automatic
replacement of the previous item. Recheck the current account and documented limit before acting.

For an organization, use its Page admin view: **Edit page > Featured**, then the available
**Manage featured** or **Feature a post** control and select the intended post. A post menu offering
**Feature on top of my profile** targets the member profile, not the organization Page. Never infer
the destination from the post's author. Verify the saved item in the Page's featured list and in
its member-facing Home tab.

## Keep guidance current

Each evolved social rule applies to all platforms where it is relevant. Assess applicability for
each destination; do not leave a reusable rule tied to the platform where it was discovered.
Record a platform-specific exception only for an actual supported difference, with its evidence.
Do not assume matching features, account capabilities or limits. Broader rule applicability never
widens authorization to publish, follow, connect or send.

Read both this guide and the project's guide at the start of the applicable work. Before finishing,
save each new approved rule or demonstrated reusable lesson in the document that owns it. Project
voice, people, services, account choices and event records belong in the project repo. Universal
platform behavior and methods belong in the shared publishing guides. Update both when both scopes change, without
duplicating a rule. Keep case-specific values and dated observations in a case record. Distinguish an
observed successful method from a promise that the platform always behaves that way.
