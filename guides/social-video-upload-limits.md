# Social video upload limits

For drafting, account selection, name links and saved-post review, use
[social-publishing.md](social-publishing.md) alongside the project's own guide. Keep universal
platform limits here and project-specific choices in the project's repo. Read both before posting
and update the owning guide when new evidence or approved practice changes it.

Regular video posts, using the platforms' own docs. Advertising and live broadcasts are outside this table.

Bluesky added October 20, 2026. LinkedIn, X and YouTube sources revisited October 20, 2026. Facebook, Instagram and TikTok entries retain their October 15, 2026 source review.

## Details that affect posting

- **Bluesky:** The official August 25, 2026 announcement confirms 10 minutes and 300 MB. The earlier [June 4 announcement](https://bsky.app/profile/bsky.app/post/3mni6hmaff22u) raised file size from 100 MB to 300 MB.
- **X:** Premium on Android allows 10 minutes. The [longer-video help](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/premium-longer-videos) conflicts on resolution: its summary says 4 hours at 1080p, while its detailed instructions require 720p above 2 hours and below 4 hours. Both give a 16 GB cap.
- **Facebook:** The [current Reel help](https://www.facebook.com/help/2862139500770200/) says videos became Reels in September 2025 and can be any length or orientation. This describes the format; it does not establish an unlimited upload size. Meta also describes the removal of Reel length restrictions in its [official announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/).
- **Facebook Stories (*):** The [Page Stories publishing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/video-api/page-stories-api) conflict: the specifications list 3–90 seconds, but the limitations say no more than 60 seconds. Use 60 seconds for this publishing route. This does not establish the limit for every phone-app Story flow.
- **Instagram:** [Reels longer than 3 minutes](https://help.instagram.com/2720958398006062) are not recommended to new audiences. The 20-minute upload allowance and the 3-minute recommendation threshold are different limits. Longer Story videos can be split into clips.
- **YouTube (†):** Shorts must be square or vertical. The [256 GB cap](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/71673?hl=en) is YouTube's general upload ceiling, not a separately stated Shorts-specific allowance. Shorts still have the 3-minute duration cap. A Short longer than 1 minute with an active copyright claim is blocked globally.
- **TikTok:** [Recording inside the app](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/camera-tools) allows 10 minutes; uploading an existing file allows 60 minutes. The [computer upload docs](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-tools-on-tiktok) specify 30 minutes and under 10 GB.

## A 3-minute video on free X

The published video must be no longer than 2 minutes 20 seconds. X's [phone-app instructions](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-videos) describe dragging the ends of the trimming bar before posting. Choose the 2:20 section you want to keep; automatic cutting at 2:20 is not documented.

## Updating this guide

Open the linked platform sources before changing a limit. Keep the upload route and account type attached to each figure. Record the actual source-review date, keep unresolved conflicts visible, and leave missing limits as “Not stated.” Do not substitute ad specifications or a scheduler's limits for normal posting limits.
