# Contributing

Keep a skill useful outside its author's own machine. Preserve the instructions, examples and supporting files that affect its behavior. Describe the tools or project inputs it requires; never imply that installing a skill grants permission to spend, publish or change access.

## Before proposing a change

1. Read the whole skill or guide and the references it uses. Change the rule that owns the subject rather than adding a second version elsewhere.
2. Use public or clearly fictional examples. Remove account details, private business notes, machine-specific paths, credentials and private history.
3. Establish who owns the material and what its license permits. Keep required notices and attribution. Publicly visible does not always mean freely redistributable.
4. Include every supporting file the change needs. Update the catalog and the declared collection in [collection.json](collection.json); give each source item a destination or a reason it is not included.
5. Run `python3 scripts/check_collection.py`. Then read the full change for privacy, preserved meaning and practical usability. The automatic checks do not establish that content is safe or accurate.
6. Open a pull request describing the changed behavior, its evidence and any limits. Do not include private evidence in public discussions.

Third-party skills listed in the catalog stay with their publishers. Link to an official source and its terms rather than copying an installation from a private workspace.

Changes to a private working setup do not publish here automatically. Each public update is selected and reviewed.
